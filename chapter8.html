Quran Root Method

Chapter 8

أَوْزَان (Verb Forms)

  • • Discover how roots "Upgrade" their meaning by moving into different Pattern Families.
  • • Master all 10 Verb Forms (from 1 to 10) that define the structure of Arabic verbs.
  • • Learn the "Meaning Secret": how adding one or two letters changes a simple action into something more powerful.
