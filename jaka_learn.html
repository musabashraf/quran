What Keeps Stakeholders Awake?

The education crisis is real. See how Jaka Learn tackles the core concerns keeping both parents and school management up at night.

🎓

The "Degree without a Job" Crisis

Parents are spending lakhs on traditional schooling and coaching, yet nearly half of all graduates remain unemployable. The traditional formula—memorize, score 95%, apply—is broken. Companies want skills, not mark sheets.

The Threat High coaching debt, zero practical skill set.
🤖

The Post-AI Panic

By the time current school children graduate, AI will have completely automated standard corporate tasks. Parents see the threat but have no resources to teach their children what it takes to remain irreplaceable.

The Threat Graduating into automated, obsolete careers.
🧠

The "Cognitive Off-Loading" Trap

With AI shortcuts everywhere, students copy and paste assignments. Parents see their children losing basic analytical ability, critical thinking, research skills, and standard independence.

The Threat Passive learning and loss of creative output.
🧗

The Fear of a One-Track Mind

Extreme pressure centered entirely on competitive exams creates intense fear. If a child fails a single test, parents worry their entire future is lost. They need alternatives like startup skills and global capability.

The Threat Exam failure leading to total career paralysis.
🏷️

The Rote-Learning Stigma

Progressive schools strive to market themselves as modern, but their actual core curriculum remains stuck in standard, outdated formulas. They risk losing enrollment to newer alternative or international boards.

The School Danger Loss of status, premium pricing, and student retention.
⏳

Teacher Burnout & Admin Drown

Teachers spend up to 40% of their energy on repetitive data entry, reporting compliance, and manual grading instead of high-impact instruction. This reduces classroom focus and increases turnover.

The School Danger Overworked faculty, lower teaching standards.
🖥️

The "Tech for Show" Problem

Smartboards and basic IT labs are bought to look modern, but schools lack a structured syllabus or actual instruction model for modern skills like AI integration, finance, or business development.

The School Danger Expensive hardware left unused or underutilized.
🛡️

The AI Policy Vacuum

Administrators know students are secretly using AI to copy work. Yet, schools lack the framework, training, and policy to direct AI from a cheating tool into an interactive, structured study partner.

The School Danger Loss of integrity and academic quality.
